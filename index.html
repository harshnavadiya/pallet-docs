Set up Pallet

Pallet is a Shopify theme for stores that sell to trade buyers and the public from one catalog. Signed-in trade buyers see their company, their prices, volume breaks and order rules, and can order by SKU in bulk. Everyone else gets a normal, fast storefront.

  • Built for wholesale and retail in one store
  • Uses Shopify's own B2B: companies, catalogs, quantity rules
  • Presets Pallet, Larder, Showroom

Getting started

  1. Add the theme in Online Store > Themes and pick a preset: Pallet for hardware and site supplies, Larder for food and drink, Showroom for beauty and apparel. Presets only change colors, fonts and the starting home page.
  2. Turn on new customer accounts in Settings > Customer accounts. Trade buyers sign in through them.
  3. Set up your trade buyers as companies with catalogs. See Trade buyers.
  4. Create three pages in Online Store > Pages and pick their templates: Buying desk (template page.desk), Apply for a trade account (template page.apply, handle trade-account) and Contact (template page.contact).
  5. Add them to your menus in Online Store > Navigation. The buying desk usually goes in the main menu, the application page in the footer.
  6. Review Theme settings > Trade and wholesale: what guests see, order minimums and the free-delivery threshold. See Theme settings.

Every trade feature reads Shopify's own data and hides itself when that data isn't there, so the store works as a normal retail store from the first install.

Trade buyers

Pallet uses Shopify B2B. You set everything up in the Shopify admin; the theme shows it.

Set up in the adminWhat trade buyers see
Customers > CompaniesThe trade strip above the header: company name, the location they're buying for (with a switcher when they have several), their terms and progress to free delivery.
Catalog with a price listTheir price on every product and in the collection table, with your retail price (the compare-at price) and the saving beside it.
Volume pricingA price-break ladder on the product page and in the collection table. The tier that applies to the chosen quantity is highlighted, and the add button shows the running total.
Quantity rulesSteppers that start at the minimum and move in the increment, a note under each stepper, and the order pad rounding pasted quantities up to fit.
Payment termsCheckout shows the terms; the cart notes that they're paying on account. To show terms in the trade strip, fill in the pallet.terms field on the company or location (see Product data).

Giving a buyer access

Add the buyer as a contact on the company, with a role at each location they may order for. When they sign in, Shopify asks which location to buy for, and the trade view appears.

Order minimums

Set Order minimum for trade buyers and, if you want, a higher First-order minimum in Theme settings > Trade and wholesale. The cart shows how far short the order is and holds checkout until it's met. Retail shoppers aren't affected.

Trade applications

The Apply for a trade account page uses Shopify's contact form, so applications arrive in your store email. Approve a buyer by creating their company and contact in the admin.

Subscriptions and gift cards are for retail checkout only. Shopify's B2B checkout takes one-time purchases, so the subscription picker hides itself for trade buyers.

Retail and guests

Choose what visitors who aren't signed in see in Theme settings > Trade and wholesale > Prices for visitors who are not signed in:

  • Show retail prices: a normal retail store.
  • Show retail prices with a trade sign-in prompt: retail prices, with a link inviting trade buyers to sign in for theirs.
  • Hide prices until sign-in: for trade-only stores. Prices, including in search suggestions, show "Sign in to see prices" instead.

Home page sections have a Show to setting (everyone, retail only or trade only), so the home page can speak to both audiences.

Product data

These optional fields add detail for trade buyers. Create them in Settings > Custom data with the namespace and key shown, then fill them in on each product. Anything left empty simply doesn't show.

FieldTypeWhere it shows
pallet.pack_labelSingle line textBeside the price and on the add button, for example "100 pack". Can be set on the product or on a variant; the variant's wins.
pallet.moq_noteSingle line textNext to stock, for example "Trade: minimum 5 packs".
pallet.lead_timeSingle line textShown when stock runs out and the item can be backordered, for example "7 days". Product or variant.
pallet.low_stock_atIntegerStock level at or below which "low stock" shows. Default 10.
pallet.specJSONThe specification table: a list of {"name": "…", "value": "…"} rows.
pallet.datasheetFileA datasheet download beside the SKU.
pallet.sample_variantVariant referenceAn "Add sample" button on the product page.
pallet.documentsRich textA collapsible block that can show any field in the pallet namespace; set its key in the block.
pallet.terms (company or location)Single line textTerms in the trade strip and on the buying desk, for example "Net 30". The location's wins.
pallet.rep (company or location)Single line textThe buyer's account manager on the buying desk.

SKUs and barcodes come from your variants. Give every variant a unique SKU; the order pad and header search match them exactly.

Buying desk and order pad

The buying desk page is a trade buyer's home: the order pad, reorders from past orders, their locations and any downloads you add.

Order pad

Buyers paste lines from a spreadsheet, one SKU and quantity per line, or type them. Each line gets a status:

  • Ready: found, and the quantity fits the rules.
  • Rounded: found, and the quantity was raised to the minimum or the next increment.
  • Backorder: found, but out of stock; it can still be ordered.
  • Not found: no variant has that SKU or barcode.

Change which characters may separate SKU and quantity in Theme settings > Trade and wholesale > Accepted separators.

Variant matrix

Products with exactly two options (for example size and color) get a grid on the product page: one quantity per combination, all added in one click. Turn it off in the product section with Show variant matrix.

Product page blocks

The product page is built from blocks you can reorder or remove: title, vendor, SKU, price, price breaks, inventory status, variant picker (buttons or dropdown), buy buttons, pickup availability, sample button, description, delivery note, specification table, collapsible, text, custom Liquid and app blocks.

Collection views

Collections can show as a grid of cards or a dense table for trade buyers. Buyers switch with the Grid and Table buttons; set the default in the collection section.

Everything else

  • Colors: color schemes for every section, plus the success, warning and error colors used by stock and order messages.
  • Typography: body, heading and SKU fonts, base size and heading scale.
  • Layout: page width and margin, corner radius, table density, animations and right-to-left layout.
  • Cart: drawer or page, and whether to ask for a PO number and a delivery note.
  • Product cards: image ratio, SKU, vendor and quick add.

Shopify features

  • Search suggestions in the header and the phone menu: products, collections, pages and popular searches, plus an exact match when a buyer types a SKU or barcode. Turn off in the header section.
  • Filters on collection and search pages, from the Shopify Search & Discovery app.
  • Pickup availability on product pages, when you offer local pickup in Settings > Shipping and delivery.
  • Subscriptions: a purchase options picker appears on products with selling plans from a subscription app. Retail only.
  • Gift cards, with a form to send the card to someone else on a date you choose.
  • Markets and languages: country and language selectors in the footer. The theme ships in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.
  • Dynamic checkout buttons for retail shoppers (never shown to trade buyers), product recommendations, 3D models and video in the product gallery and app blocks in the product and featured product sections.

Questions

Does Pallet need an app?
No. Everything on this page uses Shopify's own features. An optional companion app will add quotes, saved buying lists and credit balances; the theme already has a place for it on the buying desk.
A trade buyer signed in but sees retail prices.
Their customer record isn't a contact on a company yet, or their location has no catalog. Check Customers > Companies.
Products show as sold out for some buyers.
Shopify marks items unavailable when the buyer's market or delivery address has no shipping rate. Check Settings > Shipping and delivery for the buyer's country.
The order pad says "Not found" for a SKU I know exists.
The SKU must match exactly and the product must be published to the Online Store and in the buyer's catalog. Barcodes work too.
Can I sell only to trade buyers?
Yes. Set prices for guests to Hide prices until sign-in and, if you like, keep the storefront password on until launch.

Editing code

Before changing the theme's code, duplicate the theme first (Online Store > Themes > ⋯ > Duplicate) so you can go back. Styles live in assets/base.css and assets/critical.css, scripts in assets/pallet.js. Code changes aren't covered by theme support; for custom work, we suggest hiring a Shopify Partner.
